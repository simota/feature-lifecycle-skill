Method: authored adversarial cases and contract review, not live engine A/B.

## P1 — admission by late-discovery cost

If the fixed 3-of-6 count is an inadequate proxy, replacing count admission with the cost of discovering the *remaining* uncertainty after implementation, and whether proportionate pre-build work can reduce it, will admit R09–R14 and R37 despite two flags. R01–R04 remain on a bounded coding path even with three flags. Spec/design/planning/research/quality/operation-only cases do not become lifecycle-owned. Generic request wording alone does not authorize a heavyweight start. Failure: any of those outcomes reverses, or the new test admits costly-but-settled work solely because it is high risk.

## P2 — explicit autonomy is not silence or product delegation

If default AUTORUN_FULL conflates execution preference with launch authority, an explicit, invocation-scoped grant covering the goal (or expressly delegated goal selection), scope and budget will prevent R73/R74/R76 from launching implementation. R75/R78 can proceed autonomously inside the existing grant without another routine launch approval or a timed objection wait. External/destructive actions outside the grant still require the existing Ask First boundary. Failure: no response launches a run, merely selecting AUTORUN_FULL delegates product priority, or an already complete grant triggers redundant routine approvals.

## P3 — provenance without invented research

If mandatory synthetic persona counts and score-shaped outputs launder speculation into evidence, decision-driving needs labeled by provenance and support scope will keep one ticket plus twenty generated personas at one observation, preserve synthetic scenarios as hypotheses, and leave missing ICE/RICE inputs unscored rather than filling numbers. Matching synthetic demand and synthetic walkthrough will not close a demand hypothesis. A direct user-stated need remains valid intent without mandatory external market research. Failure: a synthetic reaction is reported as observed user behavior, a missing input gains an invented number, or a narrow user-authorized experiment is blocked merely for lacking population research.

## P4 — obligation coverage and an independent meaning check

If raw traceability/conformance percentages can conceal missing important obligations, making required and decision-critical obligations primary, keeping optional gaps explicit, and tracing the spec back to authorized intent will make AC splitting, easy-AC padding, must-have demotion and scope shrinkage ineffective. Truly optional, pre-authorized deferrals can remain open without forcing 100% nice-to-have delivery. The verifier will use original intent and independently sourced oracles, not just the implementation's interpretation of its ACs. A demand error returns to P1, a spec error to P4, a design error to P5, and a code error to P6; unaffected artifacts remain usable. Failure: a renamed or split obligation improves the gate, a percentage waives a committed obligation, or every acceptance failure goes straight back to build.

## P5 — unavailable evidence does not discharge a safety obligation

If unavailable integration/recovery surfaces currently look like successful discharge, tying VERIFY/SHIP to the risk-specific evidence requirements already owned by P5 will block readiness for a destructive migration, payment/auth change or external contract whose required integration/recovery behavior has not been tested. Low-risk, reversible changes can still proceed with the existing independent E3 floor and an honest unavailable record where E5 is not necessary. `declared-impossible(reason)` remains an honest rollback state, but does not pass without verified compensating controls and explicit acceptance of the remaining risk. Failure: a reason string alone permits irreversible release, a user assertion substitutes for runtime evidence, or every reversible local change is forced to acquire a new staging environment.

## Choice limit and attribution

Synchronizing repeated contracts and explanatory docs is part of the corresponding change, not a new runtime mechanism. No new phase, gate, role, required specialist or benchmark service is planned.

The 3-of-6 comparison will report both the raw proxy and the baseline with explicit neighboring-path exclusions respected. Neither number is an observed production false-positive rate. Cost estimates will be labeled as modeled assumptions; unexecuted agent spawns, tokens, implementation cycles and product outcomes will not be reported as measured.
